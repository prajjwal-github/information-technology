Goals

  • To ensure the holistic overall development of students through various Co-curricular and extra curricular activities
  • To imbibe social awareness and responsibilities sense among the students that enrich the community.

Objectives

  • To create a competitive environment for students.
  • To boost confidence and improve technical competence among the students through various technical & non-technical event.
  • To build team work spirit by involving more number of students in organisation and management of events/symposium.
  • To develop leadership and initiative, and to inculcate a sense of responsibility.
