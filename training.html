Details of Journey:

Industrial visit was organised on 11th January, 2024 to ThinkNEXT Technologies Pvt. Ltd. located in Sector 65, Mohali(Chandigarh) industrial Estate for the students of Third Year of Information Technology. Totally 45 students along with 2 faculty and 1 Technical staff were there in the journey.


Company Profile

ThinkNEXT Technologies Private Limited, Mohali (Chandigarh) is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. ThinkNEXT is approved from Ministry of Corporate Affairs and registered under Companies Act 1956. ThinkNEXT deals in Web designing, Web development, Digital Marketing, Mobile Apps, University/College/School ERP Software, Embedded/IoT Products, TechSmart Classes, Android Mobile Apps development, Bulk SMS, Voice SMS (sms5.thinknext.co.in), Biometric Time Attendance etc. ThinkNEXT Technologies provides IT/Electronics solutions using latest technologies e.g. Smart Card (Contact Type, Contactless), NFC, Biometrics, GPS, Barcode, RFID, SMS, Auto SMS (Shortcode), Android, Cloud Computing, Web, Windows and Mobile based technologies. ThinkNEXT has its numerous clients across the globe. ThinkNEXT is Google Partner for Google Adwords, Bing Ads Accredited, Hubspot Inbound and Email Marketing, Facebook Blueprint Certified, Microsoft Bing Ads Accredited Company. ThinkNEXT has wide expertise in .NET, Crystal Reports, Java, PHP, Android, Databases (SQL Server/MySQL), Networking etc. ThinkNEXT has its own multiple Smart Card printing, smart card encoding and barcode label printing machines to provide better and effective customer support solutions. ThinkNEXT has its sister concerns/associate partners RBH Solutions (Embedded System Products and Industrial Automation using PLC SCADA).


Brief description of the industrial visit

During our industrial visit to ThinkNEXT in Chandigarh, Mr. Munish graciously welcomed us and provided a comprehensive overview of their industry. He began by highlighting the achievements of ThinkNEXT, shedding light on their significant milestones and contributions to their respective field. This set the stage for a deeper understanding of their operations and ethos. As tour progressed, Mr. Munish explored the complexities of their real-time projects, offering insights into the types of projects the company was currently undertaking. This first hand exposure allowed us to grasp the practical applications of the theoretical knowledge we had acquired in our academic pursuits. Additionally, Mr. Munish generously shared the key strategies Think Next was employing to expand its business, with a particular emphasis on the pivotal role of digital marketing.Digital marketing emerged as a cornerstone of their business strategy, as Mr. Munish elucidated its significance in reaching and engaging their target audience. He underscored how effective digital marketing campaigns not only enhance brand visibility but also serve as a catalyst for business growth by facilitating customer acquisition and retention. This emphasis on leveraging digital platforms resonated with us, highlighting the evolving landscape of modern business practices. Mr. Munish provide an overview of the educational ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system that has been developed at ThinkNEXT using .NET framework. He further added that the Educational ERP is a comprehensive software solution tailored specifically for educational institutions, ranging from schools to colleges and universities. The ERP system is a comprehensive solution designed to optimize operations, enhance collaboration, and improve outcomes across educational institutions. With its user-friendly interface, robust features, and scalability, it empowers institutions to effectively manage their resources and focus on delivering quality education. Moreover, Mr. Munish took the time to impart his personal experiences and insights gleaned from his journey as a founder. He shared anecdotes that underscored the importance of technical knowledge in navigating the complexities of their industry. His anecdotes served as valuable lessons, emphasizing the indispensable role of continuous learning and adaptation in the entrepreneurial journey. In addition to the enriching educational experience provided by Think Next, the industry also ensured our comfort and well-being by providing snacks and refreshments during our visit. This thoughtful gesture created a warm and inviting atmosphere, fostering interactions and camaraderie among the participants. As our visit drew to a close, Mr. Munish graciously arranged for a group photo with everyone present. This symbolic gesture not only served as a memento of our time spent together but also reinforced the sense of community and collaboration fostered during our visit. The group photo captured the spirit of learning and camaraderie that defined our experience at Think Next, leaving us with cherished memories to reflect upon in the days to come.
