Department: Information Technology
Academic Year: 2026–27, Semester-I
Date: 24th to 25th August 2026
Venue: PCCoE, Pune
Coordinator- Dr. Ashwini Ladekar, Dr. Mukta Jamage

The Department of Information Technology, Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCoE), Pune, in association with the Institute Innovation Council (IIC), successfully organized a two-phase “Lean Start-up & MVP” Boot Camp / Mentoring programme on 24th and 25th August 2026 under the theme Entrepreneurship and Startup.

Phase-I on 24th August was conducted by Dr. Ashwini Ladekar, who guided students on Design Thinking and Innovation, focusing on problem identification, ideation and innovative solution development.

Phase-II on 25th August featured industry experts Mr. Akshay Yadav, Head of AI Vertical, and Mr. Avdhut Vatni, Development Head, Mulsetu Agrotech Private Limited. The sessions provided practical insights into AI-driven product development, technology execution, Lean Startup methodology, MVP development and product validation.

Students actively worked on their innovative ideas and presented their work demonstrating the journey from Idea → Validation → Prototype. Expert mentoring and feedback helped students understand how innovative concepts can be refined into potential products and startup opportunities.

The programme provided valuable exposure to design thinking, innovation, prototyping, AI-enabled entrepreneurship and the startup journey, encouraging students to transform ideas into viable solutions and explore opportunities for pre-incubation and incubation.

Department: Information Technology
Academic Year: 2026–27, Semester-I
Date: 3rd to 10th August 2026
Venue: Online Mode
Coordinator- Dr. Sandhya Waghere, Ms. Alpana Borase

The Department of Information Technology, Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCoE), successfully organized a One-Week International Faculty Development Programme (FDP) on “Next-Generation AI and Industry 5.0” from 3rd to 10th August 2026 in online mode.
The FDP was coordinated under the guidance of Dr. Jayashree V. Katti by Dr. Sandhya Waghere, Ms. Alpana Borase and Organizing Team- Mrs. Babita Jane, Dr. Maya Bembde, Dr. Sapana Kolambe, Dr. Mukta Jamage, Mrs. Rakhi Pagar, Mrs. Madhuri Kethari. Total 636 participants successfully completed the programme and received certificates.
The programme featured seven expert-led sessions covering emerging areas such as Generative AI, Large Language Models, Industry 5.0, Quantum Computing, Sustainable AI, Intelligent Systems, and Practical AI Development. Eminent experts from academia and industry shared insights on securing LLMs, human–AI collaboration, quantum computing for AI applications, AI and SDGs for research impact, cognitive prediction in battery management systems, and hands-on AI development tools.
The FDP provided valuable exposure to next-generation AI technologies, human-centric AI, quantum technologies, sustainable AI research, intelligent systems, and practical AI applications, contributing to professional development in teaching, research, innovation, and emerging technology practices.
