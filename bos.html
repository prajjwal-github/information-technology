Notes
  1. The term of nominated Members shall be three years, from AY 2023-24 to AY 2025-26.

  2. Chairman shall convene a meeting of the Board of Studies at least once every six months.

  3. The Chairman may invite any Faculty Member or Experts from outside to participate/advise on specific issues.

  4. Invitees will not have voting power in case of any critical decision-making.

Functions of PCCoE Board of Studies

The Board of Studies shall recommend the following to the Academic Council:

  1. Courses of studies.

  2. Measures for the improvement of the standards of teaching and research.

  3. Any other academic matter.
