M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science is an Autonomous Postgraduate course in the discipline of Information Technology affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University, Pune. ME - IT course has started in AY 2014-15. Under autonomy in AY 2022-23, ME Information Technology course is replaced by M.Tech.(AI & DS) with new curriculum.
The curriculum is designed in a such way that it adds the technical value additions to the technical capabilities of the students and it also inculcates the research aptitude among the students.


Department of Information Technology offers Postgraduate course M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science affiliated to SPPU Pune. PCCOE being an Autonomous Institute affiliated to SPPU, the syllabus is meticulously designed by the expert faculty and approved by the Board of Studies. The Program aims to equip the students with the required skill set in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science and make them job ready.
It supports the Research, Entrepreneurship, Startups and Employability. Clustering of elective subjects leads to specialization.
In addition to the curriculum, Academic and Research environment of PCCOE flourish the overall personality of the students to succeed in their future endeavors.

M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science program is highly structured to cater the need of the industry to take on the technical challenges posed in twenty first century. The courses are modular in approach with classroom lectures, assignments and individual projects, aimed at creating leaders capable of handling the industry design projects. The objective is to consolidate the basic and advanced knowledge of Artifical Intelligence & Data Science necessary for the development of the Engineer.

Best Practices
  • Effective course delivery
  • Blending of Online Teaching and Classroom Delivery
  • Technical interdisciplinary knowledge through open electives.
  • Clustering of elective subjects leads to specialization.
  • Career opportunities through Industry Institute Interaction
  • Care and concern for students through regular counseling
  • Research guidance by promoting various R&D activities
  • Systematic and timely completion of research projects
  • Dedicated year for the Project and Internship
