As the Research & Development (R&D) Coordinator of the Department of Information Technology, I am pleased to introduce you to the vibrant R&D ecosystem within our department.
In today’s rapidly evolving world, research and development have become foundational across all professional domains. Emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Blockchain, Cyber Security and Computer Vision are reshaping every sector of society. Recognizing the immense creative potential of our students, we as faculty take it upon ourselves to nurture and guide them through a variety of R&I initiatives.
Our department is committed to fostering research aptitude and innovative thinking among students by organizing focused activities under the R&D cell. The department hosts multiple Research Clusters that foster collaboration between students and faculty with shared research interests. These research clusters regularly conduct workshops, hands-on training sessions, and technical programs to ensure continuous skill enhancement and exposure to emerging trends.
