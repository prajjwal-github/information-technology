Events Conducted

Date of the event: 25/08/2026
No. of student participants: 31
Speaker Name:Mr. Hrishikesh Dani, B.Tech. (VNIT Nagpur), Income Tax Support Officer; SSC CGL Qualifier (2024 & 2025); UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination 2025; 97 Percentile in MHT-CET LLB

Objective: To create awareness about career opportunities through government competitive examinations and guide students on systematic and effective preparation for examinations such as UPSC, MPSC, SSC CGL and other competitive examinations.
Benefit: Participants gained awareness of various government examination pathways, career opportunities, preparation strategies, time management, consistency and effective self-study practices.


The Higher Education and Competitive Exam Cell (Vijigisha), Department of Information Technology, Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune organized a guidance session on “Career Pathways Through Government Examinations” on 25 August 2026. The session focused on creating awareness among students about various government competitive examinations and providing practical guidance for systematic and effective preparation. The resource person, Mr. Hrishikesh Dani, shared his personal experiences and practical insights from his competitive examination journey, including UPSC, SSC CGL and other examinations. The session covered different government examination pathways, career opportunities, preparation planning, time management, consistency and smart examination strategies. An interactive Q&A session was also conducted, enabling students to clarify their doubts regarding competitive examinations, preparation strategies and career options. The session motivated students to explore government examinations as a potential career pathway and emphasized the importance of early planning, disciplined preparation and consistent self-study.

Program Theme: Innovation & Design Thinking
Start Date: 25/02/2026
Student Participants: 40 + 2 Faculty
Coordinator: Mrs. Mukta Jamage

An expert talk was organized by the Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC) to introduce students and faculty to important frameworks used in transforming research ideas into real-world innovations. Session delivered by Dr. Rajesh Phursule, Associate dean SDW, author of 2 books, received 4 patet grant, research funding of 7.2 Lakhs from LA Fondation Dassault Systemes , 5 Lac rupees for Development of 3D Concrete Printed Full by SIRO. The session focused on understanding different readiness levels in research and innovation, along with the processes involved in intellectual property commercialization and technology transfer. The talk helped participants learn how innovative ideas move from the laboratory stage to market-ready technologies and successful startup ventures.
