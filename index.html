About Us

Welcome to the Department of Information Technology

The Information Technology course at PCCOE began in 2001 with one clear goal: to develop high-quality IT professionals through quality education and dedicated faculty. Today the department runs a B.Tech in Information Technology and an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science under the institute's autonomous curriculum.

Students learn in well-equipped computer laboratories and classrooms with modern teaching aids. An outcome-based curriculum is supported by co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, so every graduate leaves both technically strong and ready to lead.

Outcome-based autonomous curriculum
State-of-the-art computing labs
Doctorate-led research culture
Strong industry collaboration
